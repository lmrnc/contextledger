# Guide for knowledge owners

You own the accuracy of the policies and procedures in your area. Keep the official source current and give people one place to verify it. The Review Desk at `http://127.0.0.1:5680` gives you access to your cases without opening n8n.

## When you create or update knowledge

Use the [contribution template](docs/KNOWLEDGE_TEMPLATE.md). Give the document a clear title and choose an existing topic and knowledge type. Include the conditions, units, and exceptions. Link to the official source from other tools instead of pasting an extra copy. The Review Desk records your identity at submission and the verification and next review dates at approval.

1. Connect to the Review Desk as your department owner. Open **Contribute** and choose a topic. An existing policy opens with its approved wording and fixed audience.
2. Enter the title, knowledge text, and reason for the change. Include the conditions and who should act. For a first publication, select the intended audience.
3. Click **Submit for review**. Open the receipt’s review case to track the outcome. A separate steward checks the evidence and audience. Until then, the current published version stays in force.
4. If the steward requests clarification, read the note and submit a revised proposal for the same topic. The revised proposal replaces the earlier case in the pending queue. After approval, check **Trusted context** for the published version and citation.

## When a review arrives

1. Open **Review queue** and select your case. Compare the approved wording, proposed wording and original source references.
2. **Approve** if the source proves the replacement is correct and it belongs to the same audience. **Reject** if the approved wording should stay. **Request clarification** if a date, rule, source or audience is uncertain.
3. Record a short reason with who confirmed the decision and what needs to happen next. The Review Desk retains the decision, author and timestamp.
4. Correct outdated copies after a decision. For missing ownership or metadata, fix the source and ask the platform steward to re-run ingestion. For scope changes, contact Security and the steward.

Aim to triage high-priority cases in one business day. Verify overdue policies against the original evidence before renewing their review date. A steward can generate the quality report to find outstanding issues and ingestion failures.

## Example

Support leadership proposes 48 hours for qualifying P2 escalations. The approved Notion policy and dispatch checklist say 24 hours. Read all three sources and confirm when the new window takes effect. If it is ready, approve with a note such as “Support leads confirmed 48 hours from P2 confirmation. Update the Notion policy and dispatch checklist before the next shift.” The desk publishes version 2 and keeps version 1 in history. The report lists both source copies to update.
